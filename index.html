Sermon Review

The Righteousness of God Through Faith — Romans, Dan Lian

DAY 1 — MONDAY

Theme: Receiving, Not Achieving

Key Scripture: Romans 3:21-22 — “But now apart from the law, a righteousness of God has been revealed, being testified by the law and the prophets; even the righteousness of God through faith in Jesus Christ to all and on all those who believe. For there is no distinction.”

Sermon Insight: Paul announces that God’s righteousness has been made known apart from the law and is received through faith in Jesus Christ. The sermon points to Abraham as a living example: he did not earn a standing before God; he trusted God’s promise. This is the heart of the gospel in Romans—our hope rests on what God has done, not on our ability to assemble a perfect record. Faith is not pretending that our need is small; it is bringing our whole need to the God who is righteous and gracious.

Today’s Application: Notice where you are tempted to make spiritual performance the basis of your peace. Name that burden before God, then deliberately thank Jesus that righteousness is a gift received by faith, not a prize you win.

Closing Prayer: Father, I confess how quickly I try to prove myself. Teach me to rest in the righteousness You provide through Jesus. Let faith, not fear or striving, shape the way I begin this week.
