Sermon Review

Christian Freedom — Day 3 of 6

Theme: The Strong Serve the Weak

Key Scripture: Romans 15:1–3 — “We who are strong ought to bear with the failings of the weak and not to please ourselves.”

Sermon insight: Paul gives mature believers a surprising picture of strength: the strong do not insist on their rights but carry burdens for others. Jesus is the model, because He did not live to please Himself. Christian freedom therefore has a direction—it moves outward in patience, protection, and sacrificial love.

Today’s application: Identify one right or preference you could voluntarily set aside today for another person’s good. Do it without announcing your sacrifice or demanding repayment.

Closing prayer: Jesus, You did not use Your strength for self-protection. Give me that same generous strength so that my freedom becomes a shelter and blessing to others. Amen.
