Sermon Review

The Righteousness of God Through Faith — Romans, Dan Lian

DAY 5 — FRIDAY

Theme: No Room for Boasting

Key Scripture: Romans 3:27–28

Sermon Insight: If righteousness comes through faith, boasting is excluded. That truth humbles the religiously confident and welcomes the person who feels disqualified. Nobody stands before God by a superior moral record, background, or spiritual résumé; all come through the same gracious door. The gospel therefore creates a people marked by gratitude, compassion, and unity rather than comparison.

Today's Application: Ask God to expose one way comparison has affected your words or attitudes this week. Practice gospel-shaped humility by encouraging someone without measuring yourself against them.

Closing Prayer: Merciful Father, forgive me for seeking significance by comparison. Thank You that grace levels the ground at the foot of the cross. Make me humble, grateful, and generous toward others.
