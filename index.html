Champion of Hope — Romans · Brad Cooper

DAY 1 — MONDAY

THEME: Peace with God

KEY SCRIPTURE: Romans 5:1 — “Therefore, since we have been justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ.”

SERMON INSIGHT: The sermon begins with the good news that those declared righteous by faith now have peace with God.
