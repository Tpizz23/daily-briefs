Sermon Review

Christian Freedom — Day 1 of 6

Theme: Freedom That Serves

Key Scripture: Romans 14:7–9 — “None of us lives for ourselves alone… If we live, we live for the Lord.”

Sermon insight: Dan Lian begins by contrasting the world’s definition of freedom with the Bible’s. The world often treats freedom as independence and self-assertion, but Romans 14 points to a life that belongs to the Lord. Christian freedom is not escape from every obligation; it is release from self-centered ownership so that we can gladly live under Jesus’ good authority.

Today’s application: Name one area where you have equated freedom with getting your own way. Ask Jesus how serving Him and loving someone else could become the more truthful form of freedom today.

Closing prayer: Lord Jesus, free me from the pressure to make life revolve around me. Teach me to belong to You with joy and to discover freedom in faithful service. Amen.
