Sermon Review

Day 1 — The Bible’s Greatest Chapter · Romans · Lee McDerment

Theme: No Condemnation

Key Scripture: Romans 8:1 — “There is therefore now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus.”

Sermon Insight: Romans 8 begins with a decisive word of grace: those who are in Christ are no longer under condemnation. Lee McDerment describes Romans 8 as the rousing conclusion to Paul’s explanation of righteousness through Christ, and this is the foundation beneath every benefit that follows. The gospel does not merely improve our standing; it changes the verdict over our lives. We can face sin, weakness, and failure honestly because our identity is secured in Jesus.

Today’s Application: Notice the accusation or shame that has been loudest in your mind. Answer it with Romans 8:1, then bring one specific failure to God in confession rather than hiding.

Closing Prayer: Father, thank You that Your verdict over me is found in Christ, not in my performance. Help me receive the freedom of no condemnation and live honestly before You today.
