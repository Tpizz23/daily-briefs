Sermon Review

We Have All Sinned — Romans series, Lee McDerment

DAY 1 — MONDAY

Theme: The Universality of Sin

Key Scripture: Romans 3:23 — “For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God.”

Sermon Insight: Paul does not divide humanity into respectable people and obvious rule-breakers; he places everyone under the same honest diagnosis. That is the central claim of “We Have All Sinned”: God’s judgment makes sense because sin is real and universal, and no one stands before God on personal goodness. Romans was written to a divided church, but this truth levels the ground and points every person toward the same need for a Savior.

Today’s Application: Name one way you are tempted to compare your goodness with someone else’s. Confess that specific sin to God without minimizing it or defending yourself.

Closing Prayer: God, I confess that I have sinned and fallen short of Your glory. Keep me from hiding behind comparison, and help me receive the truth with humility and hope. Thank You that my need does not disqualify me from Your mercy.
