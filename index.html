Sermon Review

The Name Above Every Other Name — Day 3 of 6

Series: Philippians | Preacher: Lee McDerment

Theme: Obedience to the Point of Death

Key Scripture: Philippians 2:8 — “He humbled himself by becoming obedient to death—even death on a cross!”

Sermon Insight: Christ’s humility was costly and concrete. He did not merely speak about serving; He obeyed the Father all the way to the cross. His example shows that humility is tested when obedience becomes inconvenient, costly, or unseen. The cross reveals both the depth of Jesus’ surrender and the depth of His love.

Today’s Application: Ask God where obedience is costing you comfort. Take the next faithful step, even if it receives no applause.

Closing Prayer: Father, thank You for the obedience of Jesus that brought me salvation. Give me courage to obey when the path is costly, trusting Your love more than my comfort. Amen.
