Sermon Review

"What Is Your Money Costing You?" · Made for Relationships · Lee McDerment

TODAY'S FOCUS: Generosity as the Gospel Shape of Money

KEY SCRIPTURE

2 Corinthians 9:6-7 — "Whoever sows sparingly will also reap sparingly, and whoever sows with blessings will also reap with blessings. Each person should give what he has decided in his heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver."

INSIGHT

The antidote to money madness is not merely budgeting better — it's the heart behind giving. Paul's words in 2 Corinthians reframe our entire approach: generosity isn't a suggestion for the spiritual elite, it's the natural fruit of a heart captured by the gospel. When we grasp that God gave His only Son while we were still sinners (Romans 5:8), our calculations about risk and sacrifice in giving get recalibrated entirely. Cheerful giving isn't about the amount — it's about the posture. A person who tithes out of guilt gives differently than one who gives out of gratitude. The sermon's mention of "true freedom as we steward money God's way" points here: generosity is how we escape the matrix of consumption and find freedom in giving.

TODAY'S APPLICATION

Identify one person or one cause you can give to this week — not out of obligation, but out of genuine gratitude for what God has given you. Set up that gift today, whether it's money, time, or a handwritten note. Let your giving be an act of worship, not a response to guilt.

CLOSING PRAYER

Lord, You gave everything on the cross while I was still self-focused. Help me to give with that same freely-given, not-compelled spirit. Break the spirit of measurement in me — "how much is enough?" — and replace it with the joy of participating in Your kingdom work. Teach me that generosity is the doorway to freedom, not sacrifice. Amen.
