Sermon Review

Dead to Sin, Raised to Life — Day 6 of 6

Theme: Keep Walking

Key Scripture: Romans 6:22 — “But now that you have been set free from sin and have become slaves of God, the fruit you get leads to sanctification and its end, eternal life.”

Sermon Insight: New life is a beginning that must be continually walked out. Romans 6 speaks of fruit, sanctification, and a direction of travel: belonging to God gradually produces a life that looks more like the One who raised us. The message is not a one-day emotional reset, but an ongoing commitment to remember our union with Christ and yield ourselves to Him. Each day we return to the same gospel: dead to sin, alive to God.

Today’s Application: Review the week and name one evidence of God’s grace, one area needing surrender, and one practice you will continue next week. Share your commitment with a trusted believer so the change is sustained in community.

Closing Prayer: Father, keep me close to the gospel I have remembered this week. When I stumble, lead me back to confession and faith rather than despair. Help me keep walking with Jesus, trusting You to grow the fruit of a life raised with Christ.
