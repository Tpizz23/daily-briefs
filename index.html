Sermon Review

To Live is Christ, To Die is Gain — Day 3 of 6

Series: Philippians | Preacher: Dan Lian

Theme: Joy Beyond Circumstances

Key Scripture: Philippians 1:18 — “And because of this I rejoice. Yes, and I will continue to rejoice.”

Sermon Insight: Paul’s joy was resilient because it rested on Christ, not on approval, comfort, or control. Even when motives around him were mixed, he could rejoice that Jesus was proclaimed. This is everyday confidence: a durable joy that survives changing conditions because its source does not change.

Today’s Application: Notice what has been controlling your emotional state this week. Name one unchanging truth about Jesus and deliberately give thanks for it.

Closing Prayer: Lord, teach me a joy that is deeper than my mood or circumstances. Turn my attention from what shifts around me to Your unchanging presence and saving work. Amen.
