Sermon Review

DAY 6 — SATURDAY

Theme: Nothing Can Separate Us

Key Scripture: Romans 8:38-39 — “Neither death nor life… nor anything else in all creation, will be able to separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus our Lord.”

Sermon Insight: The chapter ends where Christian security must end: in the unbreakable love of God in Christ. Romans 8 gathers life and death, present and future, spiritual and earthly powers, and declares that none can separate the believer from Jesus. This is the foundation for courage and hope in a chaotic culture. As this week closes, the invitation is not to manufacture certainty but to rest in the certainty God has already given.

Today's Application: Review the six days and identify the fear that most needs the promise of Romans 8:38-39. Memorize the final verse or send it to someone who needs encouragement.

Closing Prayer: Lord Jesus, let Your unbreakable love be the deepest truth I carry forward. When life feels unstable, keep me rooted in the grace, hope, and belonging You have secured for me.
