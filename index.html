Sermon Review

Why Friendship is So Hard — Day 5 of 6

Theme: The Freedom of True Friendship

Key Scripture: Proverbs 27:9 — “Perfume and incense bring joy to the heart, and the pleasantness of a friend springs from their heartfelt advice.”

Sermon insight: True friendship brings freedom from performance, honesty, and room to grow because trusted friends know us deeply and still love us. Fear of rejection or vulnerability can cost more than the risk, while healthy friendships offer mutual encouragement and heartfelt counsel.

Today’s application: Identify where fear has kept you from genuine friendship; ask God to replace it with trust, then take one small vulnerable step with a safe person.

Closing prayer: Father, thank You for the freedom found in genuine friendship. Free me from fear, performance, and hiding; give me courage to be known and grace to receive honest counsel. Amen.
