I Can Do All Things — Day 6 of 6

Theme: The Secret Is Jesus

Key Scripture: Philippians 4:13, 19-20 — “I can do all this through him who gives me strength.”

Sermon insight: The secret of contentment is not a technique, a budget, or better circumstances; it is Jesus Himself, whose strength makes faithful living possible in every setting.
