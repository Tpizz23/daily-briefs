Sermon Review

Christian Freedom — Day 6 of 6

Theme: A Life Shaped by Love

Key Scripture: Romans 13:8–10 — “Let no debt remain outstanding, except the continuing debt to love one another.”

Sermon insight: Romans 14’s practical freedom rests on the larger command to love. Love does not erase truth or conscience; it fulfills the law by seeking another person’s good. As the week closes, Christian freedom looks like a settled posture: secure in Christ, humble about preferences, and ready to serve.

Today’s application: Choose one concrete act of love that costs you convenience today. Let it be a visible reminder that freedom in Christ is not self-indulgence but the ability to give yourself away.

Closing prayer: Father, thank You for freeing me through Christ. Make love the governing shape of my choices, and teach me to use every freedom for the good of others and the glory of Jesus. Amen.
