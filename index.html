Sermon Review

“When Screens Take Over” · Made For Relationships · Joey Odom

DAY 5 - FRIDAY

THEME: The Freedom of Intentional Living

KEY SCRIPTURE

Galatians 5:1 — “It is for freedom that Christ has set us free. Keep your standing firm, and do not be subject again to a yoke of slavery.”

SERMON INSIGHT

Odom's message carried a tone of genuine hope rather than shame. The point was not to make us feel guilty about our phones but to recognize that we can actually choose a different path. Paul's letter to the Galatians speaks of freedom not as an excuse to indulge but as a position of security in Christ that allows us to say no to lesser things. When we are truly satisfied in our relationship with God, the gravitational pull of endless scrolling, notifications, and digital distraction loses its grip. The freedom Odom described was not the freedom to use technology without limit but the freedom to use it with intention -- to be its master rather than its servant. This is the kind of freedom that makes relationships deeper and families stronger.

TODAY'S APPLICATION

Identify one specific way your technology use has stolen joy or closeness from a relationship. Without spiraling into guilt, make a concrete plan for reclaiming that moment. For example: if you have been missing evening conversations with your spouse because of scrolling in bed, commit to a device-free bedroom routine this weekend. Share your plan with someone who can check in on your progress.

CLOSING PRAYER

God, You have set me free in Christ -- free from the power of sin, and free from the need to find my worth in the approval of strangers online or the dopamine hit of a notification. Help me to walk in that freedom today by making one deliberate choice to be present for someone You have put in my path. Thank You that freedom is not the absence of boundaries but the presence of love. Let me live this day as a person who is free and full, not enslaved to a screen. Amen.
