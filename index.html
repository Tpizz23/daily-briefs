Sermon Review

"What Will You Leave Behind?" · Made for Relationships · Dan Lian

TODAY'S FOCUS: Sustaining the Investment for a Lifetime

KEY SCRIPTURE

Deuteronomy 31:8 — "Yahweh himself is who goes before you. He will be with you. He will not fail you nor forsake you. Don't be afraid. Don't be discouraged."

INSIGHT

The final day of this review confronts the long view: relational investment is not a six-week sprint but a decades-long calling. Deuteronomy 31:8 was God's word to Joshua as he prepared to lead Israel after Moses' death — a clear picture of legacy transition. God does not say 'Moses did his part, now you finish.' He says 'I will go ahead of you.' Legacy is not a baton passed in isolation; it is carried forward by the same God who was faithful to the previous generation. Dan's sermon acknowledged that the biggest threat to long-term relational investment is discouragement — when the person you are investing in makes poor choices, drifts, or seems indifferent. Deuteronomy 31:8 meets that discouragement directly: God goes ahead. He does not leave the work to us alone. The sustainable posture for legacy investment is not heroic effort but faithful, Spirit-dependent endurance — showing up again and again, even when results are invisible, trusting that the harvest belongs to God.

TODAY'S APPLICATION

Review the six days of this devotional and identify one person you want to begin or continue investing in. Commit to one specific, sustainable next step — not a dramatic gesture but a faithful rhythm (e.g., a monthly coffee, a weekly text, a daily prayer list). Ask a friend to hold you accountable to this commitment.

CLOSING PRAYER

Lord, You are the faithful God who goes ahead of me. When I grow weary in relational investment — when I feel like I am not making a difference — remind me that the outcome belongs to You. Give me endurance that is not rooted in my own strength but in Yours. I commit these relationships to You: the ones I am starting, the ones I am continuing, and the ones You will bring in the years ahead. Do not let me quit. In Your name, Amen.
