Sermon Review

The Righteousness of God Through Faith — Romans, Dan Lian

DAY 4 — THURSDAY

Theme: A Humble Confidence

Key Scripture: Romans 4:20–22

Sermon Insight: Abraham grew strong in faith as he gave glory to God, fully convinced that God was able to do what He had promised. Biblical faith is neither passive resignation nor confidence in the self. It is humble confidence in the character and power of God. In a world marked by instability—the setting Romans addresses—faith makes God’s reliability the steady place from which we live.

Today’s Application: When anxiety rises today, pause before reacting. Say aloud what is true about God’s character—faithful, able, present, and good—then choose one response that reflects trust rather than control.

Closing Prayer: Faithful God, You are able to do what You promise. Replace my need to control outcomes with a steady confidence in You. May my choices today give You glory.
