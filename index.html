Sermon Review

Dead to Sin, Raised to Life — Romans — Clayton King

DAY 1 — MONDAY

Theme: A New Identity

Key Scripture: Romans 6:4 — “We were buried therefore with him by baptism into death, in order that, just as Christ was raised from the dead… we too might walk in newness of life.”

Sermon Insight: The sermon’s central picture is baptism: union with Jesus in His death and resurrection. Romans 6 does not treat sin as a minor habit to manage; it describes a decisive change in identity for everyone who belongs to Christ. The old life is buried with Jesus, and resurrection life is now the believer’s direction. Clayton King’s message calls us to see baptism not merely as a past ceremony, but as a living reminder of who we are.

Today's Application: Name one old pattern you have continued to treat as your identity. Today, thank God that it has been buried with Christ, and choose one concrete step that reflects your new life.

Closing Prayer: Lord Jesus, I confess that I often define myself by my failures and old patterns. Help me believe that my truest identity is found in You, dead to sin and alive to God. Teach me to walk today in the newness You have given me.
