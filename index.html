Sermon Review

We Have All Sinned — Day 4 of 6

Theme: Honest Repentance

Key Scripture: 1 John 1:9 — “If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us.”

Sermon insight: Grace does not make honesty unnecessary; it makes honesty safe. Because Christ is the Savior, we can confess sin without bargaining, performing, or pretending. The Romans message moves us from the general statement “everyone sins” to the personal question “where have I sinned?” Confession opens the door to forgiveness and begins the Spirit’s cleansing work in places we would rather conceal.

Today’s application: Set aside five quiet minutes to confess specific sins—not vague shortcomings—to God. If your sin harmed someone, identify one humble step toward repair.

Closing prayer: Father, I am tired of managing appearances. Give me the humility to confess specific sin, the faith to believe You forgive, and the willingness to make things right where my choices have caused harm.
