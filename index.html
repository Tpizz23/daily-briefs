Sermon Review

Dead to Sin, Raised to Life — Romans — Clayton King

Day 3 of 6 — Wednesday

Theme: United With Christ

Key Scripture: Romans 6:5 — “For if we have been united with him in a death like his, we shall certainly be united with him in a resurrection like his.”

Sermon Insight: The answer to sin is not self-improvement detached from Jesus; it is union with Jesus. His death is where the old dominion is broken, and His resurrection is the source of the new life we are called to live. The sermon’s baptism language makes this personal: believers are not spectators of Christ’s story but participants in it. Because Jesus lives, obedience is not an attempt to earn life—it is the fruit of receiving life from Him.

Today’s Application: Spend a few quiet minutes thanking Jesus for specific ways His resurrection life has already changed you. Then ask where you need to rely on His life rather than your willpower.

Closing Prayer: Jesus, I need more than determination; I need Your life at work in me. Thank You that I am united with You in death and resurrection. Make Your resurrection power visible in the place where I feel weakest.
