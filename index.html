Sermon Review

Dead to Sin, Raised to Life — Romans — Clayton King

DAY 2 — TUESDAY

Theme: Sin No Longer Rules

Key Scripture: Romans 6:11-12 — “So you also must consider yourselves dead to sin and alive to God in Christ Jesus. Let not sin therefore reign in your mortal body…”

Sermon Insight: Paul explains the consequence of being united to Christ: sin may still be present, but it is no longer the rightful ruler. The Roman Christians lived amid pressures that could pull them back toward old loyalties, and modern believers face the same conflict in different forms. The gospel gives a new way to interpret temptation—not as proof that nothing changed, but as a moment to live from what Christ has already accomplished. Grace changes the believer’s allegiance.

Today's Application: When temptation comes, say aloud or write, “Sin is present, but it is not my master.” Identify the specific choice that would put that truth into practice today.

Closing Prayer: Father, I feel the pull of sin and sometimes mistake its strength for authority. Remind me that Jesus is my King, and that sin has no rightful claim over me. Give me courage to obey in the next small decision.
