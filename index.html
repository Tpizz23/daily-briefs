Sermon Review

To Live is Christ, To Die is Gain — Philippians · Dan Lian

DAY 1 — MONDAY

Theme: Christ Is Everything

Key Scripture: Philippians 1:21 — “For to me, to live is Christ and to die is gain.”

Sermon Insight: Dan Lian begins with the settled eternity that gives everyday life confidence. Paul does not describe Jesus as one part of a full life; Christ is the defining center of life itself. Because eternity with Christ is secure, joy is not dependent on comfortable circumstances.

Today's Application: Name the things you are tempted to treat as the main point of your life. Offer the day to Jesus with the simple prayer, “To live is Christ.”

Closing Prayer: Jesus, become the center and substance of my life today. Anchor my joy in the certainty that I belong to You now and forever. Amen.
