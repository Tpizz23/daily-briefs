Sermon Review

We Have All Sinned — Day 2 of 6

Theme: Why Judgment Makes Sense

Key Scripture: Romans 1:20 — “His invisible attributes… have been clearly perceived… in the things that have been made.”

Sermon insight: God’s judgment is not arbitrary; His power and character are evident, yet people suppress truth and turn away from their Creator. Sin is not merely rule-breaking but refusing God’s rightful authority and goodness.

Today’s application: Notice one way you resist God’s authority today—through distraction, self-reliance, or disobedience. Turn that resistance into a specific act of trust.

Closing prayer: God, I often want Your gifts without Your authority. Show me where I have suppressed what I know to be true, and give me courage to respond with reverence rather than excuses.
