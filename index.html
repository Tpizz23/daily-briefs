Sermon Review

The Power of God: A Faith that Forms — Day 4 of 6

Series: Romans | Preacher: Brad Cooper

Theme: Practical Disciplines That Strengthen Faith

Key Scripture: Romans 12:2

“Do not conform to the pattern of this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind. Then you will be able to test and approve what God's will is—His good, pleasing and perfect will.”

Sermon Insight: Romans 12 is where Paul's theology becomes intensely practical. After eleven chapters of doctrinal foundation, Paul turns to lived application: stop conforming, start transforming. The mechanism is renewing the mind. This is not passive—faith grows through deliberate engagement with God's truth. The disciplines Paul implies include regular time in Scripture, prayer, community, and worship. Each of these renews the mind and shifts us from being shaped by culture to being shaped by God's will. The sermon's emphasis on courage and hope in the gospel connects here: we cannot have courage if our minds are still conformed to the anxiety and narratives of the world.

Today’s Application: Pick one spiritual discipline you've neglected or rushed through lately (personal prayer, Bible reading, attending community group). Commit to practicing it intentionally today with full attention, not as a box to check but as a means of transformation. Note how your mind feels before and after.

Closing Prayer: Holy Spirit, renew my mind today. I want to stop absorbing the patterns of this world and start being transformed by Your truth. Forgive me for the lazy days I've spent in front of screens instead of before Your Word. Renew my thinking, God—show me what You're doing in my life and lead me into Your good will. Amen.
