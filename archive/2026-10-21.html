Sermon Review

Our Role in Evangelism/ Mission — Day 3 of 6

Clayton King | Romans series

Theme: A Heart for the Nations

Key Scripture: Romans 15:20 — “It has always been my ambition to preach the gospel where Christ was not known.”

Sermon Insight: Paul’s ambition was shaped by places and people who had not yet heard of Christ. Evangelism is more than maintaining familiar religious spaces; it carries the message toward those who are far away, overlooked, or unreached. Clayton King’s message calls us to notice the missionary direction of God’s heart.

Today’s Application: Make a short list of three people or communities who may be far from church or faith. Pray for them by name and ask God to show you one respectful bridge toward them.

Closing Prayer: Lord, enlarge my heart beyond the people who are already comfortable with faith. Give me compassion for those who do not know You and wisdom to serve them well. Amen.
