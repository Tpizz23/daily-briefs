Sermon Review

Dead to Sin, Raised to Life — Romans — Clayton King

Day 4 of 6 — Thursday

Theme: Offer Yourself to God

Key Scripture: Romans 6:13 — “Present yourselves to God as those who have been brought from death to life, and your members to God as instruments for righteousness.”

Sermon Insight: Romans 6 moves from identity to practice: those raised with Christ present themselves to God. Every part of ordinary life—our bodies, words, attention, money, and relationships—can become an instrument for righteousness. This is the practical response to the message Clayton King preached: baptismal identity must reach Monday-through-Saturday decisions. We do not defeat sin by vague intentions, but by intentionally offering ourselves to God in real situations.

Today’s Application: Choose one part of your day that you usually keep disconnected from God. Offer it to Him before it begins, and make one decision in that area as an “instrument for righteousness.”

Closing Prayer: God, I offer You my body, mind, and desires again today. Show me where I have reserved part of myself for sin or self-rule. Make even my ordinary actions useful for Your righteousness.
