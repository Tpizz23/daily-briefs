Sermon Review

The Bible’s Greatest Chapter — Day 4 of 6

Series: Romans

Preacher: Lee McDerment

TODAY’S FOCUS: Suffering and Glory

KEY SCRIPTURE

Romans 8:18 — “The sufferings of this present time are not worth comparing with the glory that is to be revealed to us.”

INSIGHT

Paul does not promise that life in the Spirit eliminates suffering. Instead, Romans 8 gives suffering a larger horizon: present pain is real, but it is not the final word. Future glory reinterprets the weight of the present without minimizing it. Christian hope is not denial; it is confidence that God is working toward a restoration so substantial that today’s troubles cannot define the whole story.

TODAY’S APPLICATION

Bring one present hardship to God without pretending it is easy. Then write one sentence about the future hope you want that hardship to be unable to steal.

CLOSING PRAYER

God of hope, meet me in the places that hurt. Keep me from despair, and anchor me in the glory You have promised even when I cannot yet see it.
