Sermon Review

"When Screens Take Over" · Made For Relationships · Joey Odom

TODAY'S FOCUS: Jesus' Rhythm of Withdrawal

KEY SCRIPTURE

Mark 6:31 — "Because so many people were coming and going, Jesus said to them, 'Come with Me privately to an isolated place and rest for a while.'"

INSIGHT

Even Jesus, who had the most important mission in human history, recognized the need to withdraw from demands — even good demands like ministry and teaching — to be with the Father and with His closest companions. The Sermon on the Mount and Jesus' frequent retreats to solitary places were not signs of irresponsibility but of covenantal trust: He believed His Father would sustain the work while He rested. Odom's prescription — intentional technology rhythms — mirrors Jesus' own pattern of stepping away from noise to be truly present. The goal is not to abandon technology but to practice the kind of deliberate withdrawal that Jesus modeled.

TODAY'S APPLICATION

Block out a 30-minute window today that is completely free of screens — no phone, no computer, no television. Use that time to sit in silence, pray, or take a walk. Practice being present with your own thoughts before reaching for stimulation. If 30 minutes feels impossible, start with 10. The goal is not perfection but a growing capacity to be quiet before God.

CLOSING PRAYER

Jesus, You show me through Your own example that withdrawal and rest are not weakness but wisdom. I confess that I have filled every quiet moment with noise — podcasts, social media, news, anything to avoid sitting with myself and with You. Teach me to rest in the way You did, trusting that My value does not depend on constant productivity or connectivity. Create in me a quiet heart that finds its peace in You alone. Amen.
