Sermon Review

The Righteousness of God Through Faith — Romans, Dan Lian

DAY 3 — WEDNESDAY

Theme: Faith Centers on Jesus

Key Scripture: Romans 3:24–26

Sermon Insight: The righteousness Paul describes is bound to the redeeming work of Jesus Christ, whom God put forward to deal with sin and demonstrate God’s justice. At the cross, God neither ignores evil nor abandons sinners; He acts decisively in mercy. Faith directs attention away from self and toward Christ.

Today’s Application: Thank Jesus specifically for bearing the weight of sin you cannot carry. If shame makes you hide, answer it with the cross by coming honestly to Christ.

Closing Prayer: Jesus, thank You that Your cross shows both God’s justice and love. Keep my eyes fixed on You, my Redeemer and righteousness.
