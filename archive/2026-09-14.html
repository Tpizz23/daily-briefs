Sovereignty and Freedom — Day 1 of 6

Theme: God Is Sovereign

Key Scripture: Romans 9:14-16 — “It does not, therefore, depend on human desire or effort, but on God’s mercy.”

Sermon insight: Romans 9–11 confronts sensitive questions about God’s sovereignty, the gospel, and Israel, presenting God as free, righteous, and merciful; His authority gives us a foundation stronger than circumstances or control.
