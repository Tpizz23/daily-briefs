Sermon Review

"Sermon: What Will You Leave Behind?"

Series: Made for Relationships · Preacher: Dan Lian

TODAY'S FOCUS: Practical Disciplines of Relational Investment

KEY SCRIPTURE

"Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly, teaching and admonishing one another in all wisdom." — Colossians 3:16

INSIGHT

Dan's message did not stay theoretical — it landed on concrete, practical ground. The sermon named several disciplines through which ordinary Christians can invest relationally in the next generation: consistent presence in someone's life (not just occasional events), the willingness to share your own failures alongside your lessons learned, and the habit of prayerfully asking who God has placed around you. Colossians 3:16 frames this as mutual teaching — 'admonishing one another' — which means legacy investment is not a one-way street from the spiritually mature to the immature. Even as we pour into others, they shape us. The disciplines that make this possible are unglamorous: showing up week after week, sending a text when you think of someone, being available for a hard conversation. The sermon's push was clear: stop waiting for a formal program and start being the person God has already called you to be in the ordinary rhythms of daily life.

TODAY'S APPLICATION

Choose one practical discipline to adopt this week: pray daily for one person younger in faith, send one encouraging text per day to someone you are discipling, or commit to showing up consistently to a space where the next generation gathers.

CLOSING PRAYER

Jesus, You showed me what it means to pour into others not with perfection but with presence. Help me stop waiting for the perfect moment or the formal program and start showing up where You have already placed me. Give me the courage to share my own story — failures included — and the consistency to keep showing up even when it's inconvenient. Amen.
