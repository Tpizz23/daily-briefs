Our Role in Evangelism/ Mission — Day 5 of 6

Theme: Obedience with Urgency

Key Scripture: Romans 15:28 — “So after I have completed this task and have made sure that they have received this contribution, I will go to Spain.”

Sermon Insight: Paul held a long-range vision, but he also acted on the task immediately in front of him. Mission grows through faithful obedience rather than vague good intentions. Today’s Application: Choose one evangelistic action you have postponed—an invitation, conversation, apology, service, or act of generosity—and take the first step today.
