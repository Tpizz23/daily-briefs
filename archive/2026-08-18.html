Sermon Review

Champion of Hope — Romans, Day 2 of 6

Theme: No Condemnation

Key Scripture: Romans 8:1 — “There is therefore now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus.”

Sermon insight: Romans moves from the verdict of justification to the freedom of a new standing before God. Because Jesus brings us to God, our past no longer has the final word and shame is not our identity. Hope grows when we believe God’s declaration more than the accusations we repeat against ourselves.

Today’s application: Notice one condemning thought that surfaces today. Answer it with the truth of Romans 8:1, then take one concrete step from shame toward faithful obedience.

Closing prayer: Father, I bring You the failures I would rather hide. Thank You that in Christ condemnation is not my future; teach me to walk as someone truly forgiven.
