Sermon Review

To Live is Christ, To Die is Gain — Day 6 of 6

Series: Philippians | Preacher: Dan Lian

Theme: A Life Worth Living

Key Scripture: Philippians 1:20–21; 2:1–5 — “Christ will be exalted in my body, whether by life or by death.”

Sermon Insight: Paul’s confidence culminates in a life aimed at exalting Christ, whether through living or dying. That purpose then takes visible shape in humility, unity, and concern for others. As this review closes, the question is not merely what we believe about eternity, but whether our lives make Christ visible today.

Today’s Application: Write one sentence describing how you want Christ to be seen through your life next week. Share it with someone who can pray for and encourage you.

Closing Prayer: Lord Jesus, be exalted in my life—in my work, relationships, choices, and limits. Since my future is with You, help me live this day with humility, courage, and love. Amen.
