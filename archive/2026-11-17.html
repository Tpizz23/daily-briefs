TODAY'S FOCUS: Waiting for the Savior

KEY SCRIPTURE

Philippians 3:20-21 — “We eagerly await a Savior… who will transform our lowly bodies.”

INSIGHT

Paul describes Christian waiting as active expectation, not passive wishful thinking. The Savior we await is powerful enough to transform what is weak, broken, and mortal. Because Christ will complete His work, believers can face weakness without shame and endure the present without losing hope.
