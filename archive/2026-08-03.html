Sermon Review

We Have All Sinned — Day 1 of 6

Theme: The Universal Problem

Key Scripture: Romans 3:23 — “For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God.”

Sermon insight: Paul does not divide humanity into the good and the bad; he places every person under the same diagnosis: all have sinned and fallen short of God’s glory. The gospel begins not with self-improvement but with honestly recognizing that none of us can claim innocence before God.

Today’s application: Where are you tempted to minimize, excuse, or compare your sin today? Name one area honestly before God instead of defending yourself.

Closing prayer: Lord, I confess that I have sinned and fallen short of Your glory. Help me stop hiding behind comparison and receive the truth with humility, knowing that my need for a Savior is shared by everyone.
