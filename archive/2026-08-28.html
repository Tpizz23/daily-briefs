Sermon Review

Dead to Sin, Raised to Life — Romans — Clayton King

Day 5 of 6 — Friday

Theme: Freedom to Obey

Key Scripture: Romans 6:14 — “For sin will not have dominion over you. For you are not under law, but under grace.”

Sermon Insight: Grace is not permission to remain captive; it is the realm where sin’s dominion is broken. The resurrection of Jesus creates real freedom—not freedom to follow every impulse, but freedom to obey God without being condemned to the old life. The sermon’s title holds both halves together: dying to sin and being raised to life. The believer can face failure honestly while still expecting grace to produce a changed walk.

Today’s Application: Where have you assumed, “This is just how I am”? Replace that sentence with a prayer for grace and take one action that agrees with God’s promise of freedom.

Closing Prayer: Lord, thank You that grace does not merely forgive my past; it frees my present. I bring You the places where I feel stuck and powerless. Help me obey You with hope, because sin is not my master.
