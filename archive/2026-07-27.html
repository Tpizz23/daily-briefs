Sermon Review

The Power of God: A Faith that Forms — Day 1 of 6

MONDAY THEME: Recognizing Our Need for Divine Power

KEY SCRIPTURE — Romans 1:16-17

> “For I am not ashamed of the gospel, because it is the power of God that brings salvation to everyone who believes... For in the gospel the righteousness of God is revealed—a righteousness that is by faith from first to last, just as it is written: ‘The righteous will live by faith.’”

SERMON INSIGHT

In Romans, Paul wrote to Christians facing a chaotic cultural moment—persecution from without and disunity from within. Yet his opening declaration is one of confidence: the gospel is not weakness, it is power. This series begins by confronting our tendency to live as if we are ashamed of the gospel or unsure of its power. Brad Cooper challenged listeners to recognize that God's power isn't distant or theoretical—it is active and present in the message of salvation through Jesus Christ. The central question is whether we actually believe the gospel has power to change us, or if we've reduced it to a moral code.

TODAY'S APPLICATION

Today, examine where you have quietly replaced the power of the gospel with your own effort. Write down one area of life where you've been trying harder instead of trusting more. Bring that area to God in prayer and ask Him to remind you of what His power actually does.

CLOSING PRAYER

God, I confess that I often live as if the gospel is good advice instead of Your power. Forgive me for the places I've tried to muscle through on my own. I believe You are powerful enough to save and to change me. Today, I rest in that power instead of my own effort. Amen.
