Sermon Review

The Name Above Every Other Name — Day 4 of 6

Series: Philippians | Preacher: Lee McDerment

Theme: God Exalts the Humble

Key Scripture: Philippians 2:9-11 — “Therefore God exalted him to the highest place and gave him the name that is above every name.”

Sermon Insight: Jesus’ exaltation followed His humiliation, and it came from the Father rather than from self-promotion. The name above every name belongs to the One who stooped lowest in love. This gives believers freedom: we do not have to manufacture our significance, because God sees faithful humility and Christ is already Lord.

Today’s Application: Release one effort to prove yourself. Let your faithfulness be enough for today, trusting God with recognition and results.

Closing Prayer: God, You exalted Jesus in Your perfect time. Help me stop grasping for recognition and rest in the security of belonging to Christ. Amen.
