Sermon Review

Governing Authorities and the Law of Love — Day 3 of 6

Romans 13 frames civic responsibility as Christian discipleship while keeping love for others as the governing ethic: love fulfills the law.

Christians are called to honor legitimate authority and contribute to social order, but submission is not blind allegiance; loyalty to God remains ultimate, and authority must be evaluated in light of Scripture.

Today’s reflection: pursue a principled, peaceable response that protects truth and seeks the good of others, especially when discussing authorities or people who disagree.
