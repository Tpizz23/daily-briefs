Sermon Review

We Have All Sinned — Day 3 of 6

Theme: The Savior We Need

Key Scripture: Romans 5:8 — “God shows his love for us in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us.”

Sermon insight: If all have sinned, then all need more than advice or a cleaner record. Paul’s message in Romans points toward the Savior whose grace meets guilty people before they can repair themselves. Jesus does not wait for us to become worthy; His death demonstrates God’s love precisely in the middle of our rebellion and failure. The answer to universal sin is not universal despair, but the good news that Christ is sufficient for every person who trusts Him.

Today’s application: Bring one failure you usually keep at a distance into the presence of Jesus. Thank Him specifically for meeting you there rather than trying to earn acceptance today.

Closing prayer: Jesus, I need You—not as an accessory to my life but as my Savior. Thank You for loving me while I was still a sinner; teach me to rest in Your finished work instead of trying to prove that I am good enough.
