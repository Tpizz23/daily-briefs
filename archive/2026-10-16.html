Sermon Review

Christian Freedom — Day 5 of 6

Theme: The Kingdom Is Righteousness

Key Scripture: Romans 14:17 — “The kingdom of God is not a matter of eating and drinking, but of righteousness, peace and joy in the Holy Spirit.”

Sermon insight: Paul re-centers the Christian life on what matters most. Secondary practices can become loud enough to hide righteousness, peace, and joy, but the Spirit forms a deeper kingdom identity in us. Freedom is not found in maximizing options; it is found in enjoying God’s kingdom and becoming a person whose life gives peace.

Today’s application: Notice what has occupied the most emotional space in you this week. Replace one secondary dispute or desire with an intentional act of righteousness, reconciliation, or gratitude.

Closing prayer: Holy Spirit, re-center me on the kingdom You are forming in me. Grow righteousness, peace, and joy in the places where I have been distracted by lesser things. Amen.
