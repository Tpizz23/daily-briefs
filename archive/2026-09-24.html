Sermon Review

DAY 4 — THURSDAY

Theme: Many Parts, One Body

Key Scripture: Romans 12:4-8 — “We have different gifts, according to the grace given to each of us.”

Sermon Insight: The living sacrifice is not isolated spirituality. Romans 12 describes a body in which different people serve through different gifts: teaching, encouragement, generosity, leadership, and mercy. Dan Lian’s message on a gospel-shaped life points toward active participation rather than spectatorship. Grace does not merely save us individually; it equips us to strengthen the people around us.

Today’s Application: Choose one gift or responsibility God has entrusted to you and use it deliberately for someone else today. Make the action concrete—a word of encouragement, practical help, generosity, or patient presence.

Closing Prayer: Jesus, thank You for making me part of Your body. Show me how to use what You have given me with humility, excellence, and love for others.
