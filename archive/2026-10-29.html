Sermon Review

Our Role in Evangelism/ Mission — Day 4 of 6

Theme: A Clear Message and Life

Key Scripture Romans 15:18 — “I will not venture to speak of anything except what Christ has accomplished through me.”

Sermon insight: Paul’s witness was not self-promotion. He pointed to what Christ had accomplished and allowed his life to confirm his message. Effective evangelism joins clear words about Jesus with a life that displays His grace.

Today’s application: Prepare a two-minute account of one way Jesus has changed your thinking, hope, or relationships. Practice saying it plainly without exaggeration or religious jargon.

Closing prayer: Jesus, keep my witness centered on what You have accomplished. Let my words be truthful and my life make the message of Your grace believable. Amen.
