Living Sacrifice — Day 6 of 6

Theme: Overcoming Evil with Good

Key Scripture: Romans 12:14-21 — “Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good.”

Sermon insight: Romans 12 ends with a way of life impossible to sustain by willpower alone; blessing enemies, refusing revenge, and meeting evil with good reflect Jesus’ mercy and trust God’s righteous judgment.
