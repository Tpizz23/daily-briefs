Sermon Review

The Power of God: A Faith that Forms — Day 3 of 6

Series: Romans | Preacher: Brad Cooper

Theme: Jesus as the Model of Faith Under Trial

Key Scripture: Romans 5:3-5

“Not only so, but we also glory in our sufferings, because suffering produces perseverance; perseverance, character; and character, hope. And hope does not put us to shame, because God’s love has been poured out into our hearts through the Holy Spirit, who has been given to us.”

Sermon Insight: While this sermon focused on Romans 1, the broader series context draws from the entire letter—including Romans 5, where Paul connects suffering to hope. The series description explicitly mentions that Roman Christians faced unprecedented persecution. Jesus is the ultimate model of faith exercised under trial: He endured the cross despising the shame. The gospel does not remove suffering, but it reframes it—suffering produces perseverance, which builds character, which deepens hope. When we understand that Jesus walked the path of faithful suffering before us, we stop expecting the Christian life to be easy and start seeing difficulty as the mechanism by which God shapes us.

Today’s Application: Identify one current trial or difficulty you’re facing. Instead of asking God to remove it immediately, ask Him to show you what He wants to produce in you through it. Write down the answer and hold onto it as an act of trust.

Closing Prayer: Jesus, You suffered and were rejected, yet You remained faithful. You did not demand an easy path—You walked through the hardest road imaginable for me. Give me the grace to follow You through difficulty, trusting that suffering is not meaningless but purposeful. Produce in me the character that only trials can build. Amen.
