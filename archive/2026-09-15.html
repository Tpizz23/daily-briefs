Sermon Review

“Sovereignty and Freedom” · Romans · Brad Cooper

TODAY'S FOCUS: Mercy, Not Merit

KEY SCRIPTURE

Romans 9:30-33 — “They pursued it not by faith but as if it were by works.”

INSIGHT

Romans explains the difference between receiving righteousness by faith and trying to establish our own righteousness. The gospel exposes our instinct to measure ourselves, defend ourselves, and earn our place. God’s mercy is not a reward for spiritual performance; it is grace received with open hands.

TODAY'S APPLICATION

Notice where you are relying on performance to feel secure with God or others. Replace one self-justifying thought with a simple confession of faith in Christ.

CLOSING PRAYER

Lord, rescue me from the exhausting need to prove myself. Thank You that Your mercy is grounded in Christ, not in my record. Amen.
