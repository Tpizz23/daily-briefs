Sermon Review

We Have All Sinned — Day 5: Freedom from Condemnation

Key Scripture: Romans 8:1 — “There is therefore now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus.”

Sermon Insight: The reality of sin is serious, but it is not the final word for those who belong to Christ. The Savior who exposes our guilt also removes condemnation and gives us a new standing before God. Freedom is not pretending the diagnosis was wrong; it is knowing that Jesus has carried the judgment we deserved. From that secure place, we can face sin honestly without being trapped by shame.

Today's Application: When shame tells you that failure defines you, answer it with Romans 8:1 and one concrete step of obedience. Let acceptance in Christ motivate change rather than fear.

Closing Prayer: Lord Jesus, thank You that my sin is real but condemnation is not my identity in You. Help me walk today as someone forgiven, free to obey, and no longer enslaved to shame.
