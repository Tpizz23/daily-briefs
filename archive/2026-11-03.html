Sermon Review

To Live is Christ, To Die is Gain — Philippians · Dan Lian

DAY 2 — TUESDAY

Theme: Confidence in Hard Places

Key Scripture: Philippians 1:12–14 — “What has happened to me has actually served to advance the gospel.”

Sermon Insight: Paul wrote Philippians from difficult circumstances, yet he could see God at work through them. His confidence was not that hardship was pleasant, but that Christ remained sovereign and the gospel continued moving forward. Dan Lian’s message invites us to measure circumstances by God’s mission rather than measure God by our circumstances.

Today's Application: Identify one frustrating circumstance. Ask how God might form courage, witness, or dependence in you through it, and take one faithful step instead of waiting for ideal conditions.

Closing Prayer: Father, meet me in the places I would rather escape. Give me Paul’s confidence that You can use even difficulty to advance Your good purposes. Amen.
