Sermon Review

"What Is Your Money Costing You?" · Made for Relationships · Lee McDerment

TODAY'S FOCUS: The True Cost of Money

KEY SCRIPTURE

Matthew 6:24 — "No one can serve two masters, for either he will hate the one and love the other, or he will be devoted to the one and despise the other. You cannot serve God and money."

INSIGHT

Jesus doesn't say money is bad — He says it's a master that demands total allegiance. When money controls our decisions, it hijacks our relationships, our time, and our trust. How does money affect our relationship with God and with others? For many of us, the answer is more troubling than we'd like to admit. Money creates anxiety, fuels comparison, and breeds resentment when it becomes the lens through which we evaluate our worth. The chaos of "money madness" isn't just about spreadsheets — it's about an idol that promises security but delivers slavery. Series context: Made for Relationships exposes how anything that competes with God for our heart's devotion fractures the relationships we're made for.

TODAY'S APPLICATION

Today, examine your relationship with money honestly. Where does financial anxiety show up in your life — in conversations with your spouse, in decisions about your time, in how you think about your future? Ask God: "What would it look like to serve You instead of this master?" Write down one money-related decision you'll make differently today as an act of worship.

CLOSING PRAYER

God, I confess that money has often been a master in my life when You deserve to be the only One. Forgive me for the times I've trusted in what I have rather than in who You are. Today I choose to serve You alone. Show me where money has created chaos in my relationships and give me the courage to release control. In Jesus' name, Amen.
