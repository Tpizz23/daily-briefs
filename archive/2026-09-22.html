Sermon Review

DAY 2 — TUESDAY

Theme: A Different Pattern

Key Scripture: Romans 12:2 — “Do not conform to the pattern of this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind.”

Sermon Insight: A living sacrifice cannot be shaped by every surrounding pressure. Romans 12 moves from surrendered bodies to renewed minds, because our beliefs and desires direct our lives. Dan Lian points listeners toward a gospel-shaped way of living in a culture that constantly forms us. Transformation is not cosmetic conformity; it is the Spirit’s deep work of teaching us to discern what pleases God.

Today’s Application: Identify one message from your culture that has been discipling you—about success, identity, or comfort. Replace ten minutes of passive consumption with Scripture and ask God to renew your mind.

Closing Prayer: Lord, expose the patterns that are shaping me without my noticing. Renew my mind through Your truth so that my choices increasingly reflect Your good and pleasing will.
