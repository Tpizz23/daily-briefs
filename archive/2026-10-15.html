Sermon Review

Christian Freedom — Day 4 of 6

Theme: What Builds the Church

Key Scripture: Romans 14:19 — “Let us therefore make every effort to do what leads to peace and to mutual edification.”

Sermon insight: The test of freedom is not simply whether something is permitted; it is whether it builds up the people around us. Dan Lian’s message calls believers to measure choices by love and the health of the community. Peace is active work, and edification requires attention to how our actions affect another person’s faith.

Today’s application: Before making a choice today, ask: “Will this help someone move toward Jesus, or will it make faith harder for them?” Choose the path that builds peace where you can.

Closing prayer: God of peace, slow me down enough to notice how my choices affect others. Make me eager to build people up rather than prove that I am right. Amen.
