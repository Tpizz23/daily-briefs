Sermon Review

Why Friendship is So Hard — Day 6: Sustaining Enduring Friendships

Key Scripture: Colossians 4:2 — “Devote yourselves to prayer, being watchful and thankful.”

The final day focuses on sustaining friendships for the long haul through prayer, attentiveness, and gratitude. Friendship is a long obedience, not a sprint; as we grow as friends, we reflect the God who has called us His friends.

Today's application: Pray specifically for existing and hoped-for friendships. Write down two or three friends to pray for this week, then reach out to one with something you appreciate about the friendship.

Closing prayer: Lord, I commit my friendships into Your hands. Sustain the friendships You have given me; give me eyes to see my friends’ needs and a heart to serve faithfully. Help me be watchful, attentive, and grateful. Amen.
