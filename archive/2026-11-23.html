Sermon Review

DAY 1 — MONDAY

Theme: Contentment in Christ

Key Scripture: Philippians 4:11-13 — “I have learned to be content whatever the circumstances… I can do all this through him who gives me strength.”

Sermon Insight: Brad Cooper teaches that Paul’s famous words are not a promise of unlimited achievement but a testimony of learned contentment. Paul had lived through abundance and need, yet neither circumstance controlled his joy. Christ gives strength not merely to accomplish more, but to remain faithful and steady when circumstances change.

Today's Application: Name the circumstance you believe must change before you can be content. Ask Jesus to meet you there today, and practice gratitude for three gifts already present.

Closing Prayer: Jesus, teach me the contentment Paul learned. Be my strength in both abundance and need, and free me from making circumstances my source of peace. Amen.
