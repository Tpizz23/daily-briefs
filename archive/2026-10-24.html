Sermon Review

Our Role in Evangelism/ Mission — Day 6 of 6

Theme: The Church as a Sending People

Key Scripture: Romans 15:30–32 — “Join me in my struggle by praying to God for me.”

Sermon insight: Paul asked the church to join his mission through prayer, welcome, and shared labor. The church is not merely a place where people gather; it is a people sent together for the sake of the gospel. As this review closes, our role is ongoing: pray, welcome, speak, give, go, and help others go.

Today’s application: Gather with one or two believers and pray specifically for someone far from God. Then agree on one concrete way your church or household will participate in gospel mission this month.

Closing prayer: Lord Jesus, make us a sending people. Bind our prayers, words, generosity, and service together so that many more people may encounter Your mercy. Amen.
