Sermon Review

Our Role in Evangelism/ Mission — Day 2 of 6

Clayton King | Romans series

Theme: Partnering in Mission

Key Scripture: Romans 15:24 — “I hope to see you in passing and to have you assist me on my journey there, after I have enjoyed your company for a while.”

Sermon Insight: Paul envisioned mission as partnership, not a solo performance. The Roman church would encourage, send, pray, and materially assist the work beyond its own neighborhood. Our role in evangelism includes the faithful support that makes gospel witness possible. Every believer can participate through presence, prayer, generosity, hospitality, or direct witness.

Today's Application: Identify one missionary, ministry, or local believer doing gospel work. Send encouragement, offer practical help, or pray specifically for that person’s next step.
